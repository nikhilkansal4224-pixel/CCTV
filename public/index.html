ANDROID (GOOGLE PLAY) Verified 50M+ Downloads

IP Webcam (by Pavel Khlebovich)

  1. Open Google Play Store on your phone.
  2. Search and install IP Webcam (free, official app).
  3. Open IP Webcam, scroll to Connection settings → Login/password.
  4. Enter your chosen CCTV ID (Username) and Password.
  5. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi network as Sentinel.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Start server".
  7. Note the address shown on your screen (e.g., http://192.168.1.XX:8080).
iOS (APPLE APP STORE) Verified Safe

IP Camera Lite (by Yin Davidson) / Live-Reporter

  1. Open Apple App Store on your iPhone / iPad.
  2. Search and install IP Camera Lite or Live-Reporter (free).
  3. Go to App Settings → enable User Authentication.
  4. Set your CCTV ID (Username) and Password.
  5. Ensure your iPhone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  6. Tap the camera icon to Start Broadcast.
